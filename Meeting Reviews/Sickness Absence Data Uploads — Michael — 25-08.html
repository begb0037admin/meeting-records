Risks and no-promise guardrails

Data quality changes the estimate

Templates contain date issues, names instead of IDs, invalid date ranges, incomplete rows and unexplained colour coding. Cleaning, validation and rework may take longer than the technical load.

Validate, don’t infer

45 departments excluding SBS and GLAM show zero sickness absence, but that is an anomaly rather than proof of missing data. Do not state that the master template is entirely un-loaded until People XD/data-warehouse checks confirm it.

Do not promise

  • Do not commit to 25 September before a representative sample and Michael’s capacity are assessed.
  • Do not delegate uploads without a tested SOP, sample reconciliation and technical sign-off.
  • Potential data-governance/GDPR concern is not a proven breach.
  • Survey participation is for senior leadership to decide from the facts.
