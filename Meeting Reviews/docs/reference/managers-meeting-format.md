## Roadmap rule

Every active Roadmap item must be assessed. Include an item in the spoken brief
when it is blocked, overdue, materially changed, carrying delivery risk,
externally dependent, close to a significant milestone, requiring management
awareness/escalation/decision, or strategically important.

Do not copy the Roadmap meeting wholesale. Retain an inclusion or exclusion
reason for every active item in the structured `roadmapAssessment`.

## Safety rule

This format is draft-only. It never authorises Outlook refresh, source-system
updates, a repository write, publication, sending, distribution or scheduling.
