# Meeting Reviews — Project Summary

**Location:** Claude Projects / Meeting Reviews
**Last updated:** 22 April 2026

---

## What this project is for

A running archive of meeting reviews pulled directly from Granola. When Kevin asks for a review, Claude reads his Granola meetings for the requested period, summarises each one, extracts actions, and saves the output here.

---

## How to trigger a review

Say any of the following:
- "Review my meetings this week"
- "Give me a meeting review for last week"
- "What happened in my meetings yesterday?"
- "Catch me up on my meetings"

Claude will use the Granola MCP connector to pull notes and transcripts, then produce and save a review.

---

## What a review covers

For each meeting:
- Title, date, attendees
- Summary (2–4 sentences)
- Key decisions made
- Actions assigned (with owner where visible)
- Any follow-ups needed

Followed by a consolidated action list across all meetings.
