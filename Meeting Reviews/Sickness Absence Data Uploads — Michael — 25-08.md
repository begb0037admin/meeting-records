**Purpose:** Get a defensible, caveated estimate today for Marie’s resource ask — not a delivery commitment.

**Current state:** Kevin has asked Michael for the best time to catch up today about the uploads and likely effort, because Kevin needs to feed back to Marie later today. Await Michael’s reply; do not send a duplicate follow-up.

## What is known

- SBS, Paediatrics and GLAM are not yet migrated to People XD.
- A historic/backdated “Upload 2” was not sent to Michael or loaded. A master template has about 2,859 rows, but its actual load status must be checked against People XD/data warehouse.
- The source templates contain data-quality issues: dates, names in place of IDs, invalid date ranges, incomplete rows and unexplained colour coding.
- 45 departments (excluding SBS and GLAM) show zero recorded sickness. That is an anomaly to validate, not proof that their data is absent.
- Working target is data loaded around 25 September, before the survey is expected around 9 October. Kevin and Sarah still need to confirm the exact target.
- Marie is seeking BA capacity, potentially funded through SBS and Paediatrics, and needs an estimated duration/role scope for David White today.

## 15-minute structure

### 1. Set the outcome — 1 minute

**Say this:**

*“I need to give Marie a realistic resource picture today. I don’t want to turn an early estimate into a commitment, so can we separate loading from data cleaning and validation?”*

### 2. Establish the scope — 4 minutes

Ask:

1. What files/templates are actually in scope, and what is their approximate row count?
2. Can we first confirm whether Upload 2 has already been loaded, wholly or partly?
3. Which quality issues prevent loading, and which can be corrected through a repeatable rule?

### 3. Get an effort and capacity estimate — 5 minutes

Ask:

1. For one representative template, how long for intake/format checks, ID/reference matching, load, reconciliation, error correction and QA?
2. What is the estimate per template or per 1,000 rows, and what assumptions make it change?
3. Given your current commitments, what can you do today and what is the realistic completion range/date?

### 4. Test the resource model — 3 minutes

Ask:

1. Could a BA/administrator do any steps safely using a tested SOP?
2. Which steps must remain with you for technical validation, correction or sign-off?
3. What training, sample check and reconciliation would be needed before delegation?

### 5. Close with the usable answer — 2 minutes

**Say this:**

*“I’ll tell Marie this as a range with the assumptions and dependencies attached. Before we make a firm commitment, we need a representative template checked and the historic-load status confirmed. What is the clearest one-line position I can use today?”*

## Output Kevin needs to capture

- Estimate, basis and confidence level.
- Michael’s capacity and earliest realistic completion range.
- Delegable steps, technical controls and named sign-off.
- Dependencies/blockers and owner for each.
- Provisional one-line update for Marie.

## If Michael cannot meet today

Do not invent an estimate. Tell Marie only that the estimate is being validated with Michael today; the constraints already known are data quality, unknown historic-load status and the need to confirm scope. Escalate only for a decision on whether to proceed with a provisional BA ask without a validated technical estimate.

## Risks — what not to promise

- Do not promise 25 September until a representative sample and Michael’s capacity have been assessed.
- Do not quote “load time” as end-to-end time; cleaning, validation and rework may dominate.
- Do not delegate uploads without a tested SOP, sample reconciliation and technical sign-off.
- Treat the potential data-governance/GDPR issue as a concern for proper escalation, not as a proven breach.
- Survey participation is for senior leadership to decide from the facts.
