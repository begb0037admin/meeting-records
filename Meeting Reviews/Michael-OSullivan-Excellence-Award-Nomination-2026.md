## Criteria (exceptional performance)

Michael O'Sullivan has delivered an exceptional body of work over the past twelve months, combining the successful completion of a multi-year strategic programme with sustained high-quality BAU delivery across the full breadth of his role.

The centrepiece of this year's contribution is the completion of the Workforce Management Leave and Absence programme — the culmination of a sustained multi-year delivery touching thousands of staff across the University. Beginning with WFM Timesheets and Absence (February 2025), WFM Rolled Up Holiday Pay (March 2025), and Wave 2 (July 2025), Michael continued through WFM Employee Calendar View and WFM Holiday Period End 25/26 (both January 2026), before bringing Wave 3 to go-live on 9 June 2026 — closing out the programme entirely. Michael was the lead functional analyst throughout. Anna Carter-Windle, Business Change Manager at IT Services and project lead, described his contribution: *"I would have been lost without him on this project — without him we wouldn't have achieved anything. He works harder than anyone I know and always puts in the hours without complaint to get a job done."*

For the second consecutive year, Michael was the named lead senior functional analyst for the University's pay award cycle, responsible for configuring standard scaled rates and scaled allowances across PeopleXD. In 2025 this covered the clinical pay uplift and the non-clinical UCEA award plus an increase to the Oxford University Weighting. This year carried additional complexity with the UCEA clinical pay uplift targeting August 2026 payroll, requiring coordination across PeopleXD and the X5 Research Services system.

Michael also drove the TIMDEP Leave and Absence reporting suite to go-live in February 2026, resolving outstanding issues that had been causing operational problems for departments across the University. His expert knowledge of this suite resolved a live pay-impact query as recently as June 2026. Additional completions this year include: TUPE balance migration for transferring staff; OSPS pension contribution rate changes; Allow Employee Requests: Sickness Absence configuration; Non Sickness Absence Code Restructure; and NDM Workgroup Review.

What sets Michael apart is not only the volume of delivery but the quality of his engagement with colleagues and departments. When the Head of HR Systems asked Michael to assess the feasibility of a complex system change in April 2026, her response to his reply was: *"Thanks Michael, a comprehensive, clear response as always."* That phrase reflects consistency. Every department that contacts HR Systems with a complex query is better served because Michael is on the team. He is prompt, thorough, honest about what the system can and cannot do, and trusted by stakeholders at every level of the University.

Michael's contribution is exceptional in both scale and quality. He is the model of what outstanding performance looks like in a Senior Functional Analyst role, and I am delighted to nominate him for a second consecutive year.
