His work was recognised externally and unprompted: Jasmin Khokhra, a key stakeholder, wrote on 26 June 2026, *"Thanks for the update Michael and all your work on this."*

What sets Michael apart is not only the volume of delivery but the quality of his engagement with colleagues and departments. Michael's contribution is exceptional in both scale and quality. He is the model of what outstanding performance looks like in a Senior Functional Analyst role, and I am delighted to nominate him for a second consecutive year.
