Michael O'Sullivan has delivered an exceptional body of work over the past twelve months, combining the successful completion of a multi-year strategic programme with sustained high-quality BAU delivery across the full breadth of his role.

The centrepiece of this year's contribution is the completion of the Workforce Management Leave and Absence programme. Wave 3 went live on 9 June 2026, closing out a project spanning multiple years and touching thousands of staff across the University. This followed Wave 2 (July 2025), WFM Timesheets and Absence (February 2025), WFM Rolled Up Holiday Pay (March 2025), WFM Employee Calendar View (January 2026), and WFM Holiday Period End 25/26 (January 2026). Michael was the lead functional analyst throughout. He works harder than anyone I know and always puts in the hours without complaint to get a job done."*

His work was recognised externally and unprompted: Jasmin Khokhra, a key stakeholder, wrote on 26 June 2026, *"Thanks for the update Michael and all your work on this."*

Michael also drove the TIMDEP Leave and Absence reporting suite to go-live in February 2026, resolving outstanding issues that had been causing operational problems for departments across the University. His expert knowledge of this suite resolved a live pay-impact query as recently as June 2026. Additional completions this year include: TUPE balance migration for Plymouth University transferees; OSPS pension contribution rate changes; Allow Employee Requests: Sickness Absence configuration; Non Sickness Absence Code Restructure; and NDM Workgroup Review.

What sets Michael apart is not only the volume of delivery but the quality of his engagement with colleagues and departments. When the Head of HR Systems asked Michael to assess the feasibility of a complex system change in April 2026, her response to his reply was: *"Thanks Michael, a comprehensive, clear response as always."* That phrase — *as always* — reflects something consistent. Every department that contacts HR Systems with a complex query is better served because Michael is on the team. He is prompt, thorough, honest about what the system can and cannot do, and trusted by stakeholders at every level of the University.

Michael's contribution is exceptional in both scale and quality. He is the model of what outstanding performance looks like in a Senior Functional Analyst role, and I am delighted to nominate him for a second consecutive year.
